# Step 3 – Grammar labels and blanks

## What the grammar quiz asks

Every grammar point has **two questions**:

1. **Meaning** – the card shows the grammar *label* (e.g. `〜ながら`) and you type the English meaning.
   → Any English in the label gives the answer away.
2. **Fill in the blank** – the card shows the example sentence with a gap plus its English translation, and you type the missing part in kana. The label is **not** shown on this card.

## Blanks that don't test the grammar

An automatic check flagged 49 blanks, but almost all of them are just the grammar point conjugated (〜出す → 出した, ておく → おきます), which is fine. Two really test something else:

| Item | Sentence | Blank / answer | Problem | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ようと思う | 大学で働こうと思っています。 | 働こう → はたらこう | The answer is the verb 働く in volitional form. You need to know the word 働く, and the grammar part と思う isn't tested at all. | Blank the grammar instead: 大学で働こう＿＿思っています → と. Better: blank こうと思, i.e. 大学で働＿＿っています → こうとおも, which tests "volitional + と思う" together. |
| Potential form | 漢字が読めます。 | 読めます → よめます | Same kind of issue (whole verb), though here the conjugation **is** the grammar point. | Keep, but add a form hint to the card: 漢字が＿＿ (読む → potential). |
